Why do chatbots say "too many people, try again"?

Think of a small restaurant. It has 2 tables (the servers doing the real work) and a waiting line with room for only 3 people. Press the buttons and watch what happens, one slow step at a time.

One "step" is one tick of the clock: each person at a table gets a bit closer to finishing. Blue = free user, gold = paid user.

1. The line outside (waiting room: 3 spots)

People wait here when both tables are busy. Paid users stand ahead of free users.
↓ next person goes to a table when one frees up ↓

2. The tables (the AI servers answering questions)

Each person needs a few steps to finish their chat.

3. Turned away at the door

The line was full, so these people see: "Too many people, try again."
